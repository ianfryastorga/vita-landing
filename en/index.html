The difference

Four suppliers. None of them sees the whole thing.

Each one covers a piece and hands you back the rest. The gaps between them are where the money leaks.

A receptionist Answers and welcomes people in the room Leaves at closing time. Doesn't sell or follow up.
A salesperson Closes plans Doesn't run the schedule, the charges or the receipts.
A marketing agency Campaigns and content Never sees your bookings or your payments, so it optimises blind.
Booking software Stores the information Somebody still has to operate it, and that somebody is you.
Vita All of the above, run for you The bookings and the payments go through us, so the marketing is aimed at what actually converts.

The fine print

“This sounds too good to be true.”

We hear it in every first meeting, so here are the answers before you ask.

How can it cost less than the six salaries?

The platform absorbs the repetitive part and the same team works across several centers. You are not paying six salaries, you are paying your share of a team that already knows how to do this.

Is it a bot answering my clients?

Most of it is resolved automatically. What shouldn't be answered by a machine goes to a person on our side, with the full context. You define the tone before any of it goes live.

And if it doesn't work for me?

Monthly contract, no minimum term. Your data is yours and exportable whenever you ask.

What do you not do?

The class. Hiring your team. Setting your prices, though we recommend. And nothing gets published without your approval.

Do I have to migrate everything myself?

No. We run the migration ourselves, from the backup to the training. Your team doesn't fill in a spreadsheet.

And after go live?

You get the same contact every month, on WhatsApp, plus training whenever someone new joins your team.
